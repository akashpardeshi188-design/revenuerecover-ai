# AI SDR & Sales Conversion Engine

## 1. Overview
The AI SDR Agent conducts intelligent, outcome-focused conversations with inbound leads and outbound replies. It qualifies prospect fleet size, identifies missed-call revenue leakage, and overcomes objections using 12 structured playbooks.

## 2. 12 Objection Playbooks
1. **“Too expensive”:** Demonstrates how 1 single recovered repair job ($350–$1,400) pays for 3–12 months of the $119/mo Growth plan.
2. **“We already have a CRM”:** Explains that RevenueRecover AI is not a CRM replacement, but an instant webhook acceleration layer for ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and Jobber.
3. **“We already have an answering service”:** Highlights the 45-second 2-way SMS response advantage over 2-hour call center callbacks.
4. **“We don't need AI”:** Emphasizes Copilot mode, where contractors approve every message with 1 click.
5. **“Send information”:** Sends 1-page executive summary PDF with localized ROI metrics.
6. **“Need to talk to owner”:** Offers to prepare a customized 1-page revenue leak audit under the shop's name.
7. **“Not interested”:** Instantly suppresses further outreach and offers free self-service scanner link.
8. **“How does it work?”:** Explains the 3-step setup (forward calls -> 45-sec text-back -> auto booking).
9. **“Is this compliant?”:** Highlights TCPA 10DLC registration, quiet hours, and automated STOP opt-out handling.
10. **“Can I cancel?”:** Reassures zero long-term lock-in contracts with 1-click self-serve cancellation.
11. **“What happens after signup?”:** Details the 60-second provisioning process and delivery of 150 local leads.
12. **“What is the ROI?”:** Outlines the typical 24x to 35x monthly ROI across missed calls, dormant re-engagement, and invoice collections.
